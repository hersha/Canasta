Scorer

How we score it. These are house rules and differ from the standard game in a few places — the wild canasta especially. The scorer uses exactly the numbers on this page.

Wild cards

Twos and Jokers are wild.

What the cards are worth

These are the card points you count up at the end of a round: everything you have melded on the table counts for you, everything still in your hand counts against you.

Red 3s

Red 3s are bonus cards. You do not meld them or play them; they just sit there and count. Each is worth , and if you are holding or more, the whole lot doubles.

The catch: if your side finishes the round without a single canasta, your red 3s count against you instead — at the same value, doubling included. Four red 3s and no canasta is .

Black 3s

Black 3s are worth and are only ever a safe discard — they never get played down. In practice that means they only show up as points against you, in your hand at the end of a round.

Winning

First side to takes it. You can play head to head, three ways, or in partnerships.
